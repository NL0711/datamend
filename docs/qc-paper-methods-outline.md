# Paper methods outline (precipitation QC)

Status: outline only. Every number comes from `python -m qc paper` run on real outputs; none exists yet.
Companion files: `docs/qc-system-spec.md` (design), `docs/qc-evaluation-protocol.md` (standing rules, budget log).
Generated by the tool, not by hand: `<run>/paper/tables/*.md|csv`, `<run>/paper/figures/*.png`, `<run>/paper/claims.md`.

## 1. Problem and scope
- Daily gridded rainfall (RF25, India). Faults are bad cell-days in a gridded product, found without station data.
- Position: the ingredients (rules, neighbour checks, learned forecasts, federated training) are not new. The study
  is the combination, an honest comparison of its parts, and a protected-extreme test. No novelty claim for any one part.

## 2. Data and splits
- RF25, land cells, 8 clients; clients 3 and 6 held out with a 6-cell buffer. Train 1951-2012, validation 2013-2017,
  test 2018-2023, stress 1901-1950 (evaluation only). Fusion fitted 2013-2015, thresholds from clean 2016-2017.
- Per-client normalization from training years only; leakage audit result goes here.
- Figure: client map with held-out regions (`fig_client_map`).

## 3. Benchmark
- Synthetic faults, frozen and hashed: spike, stuck, scale, phantom rain, zeroed, repeat, drift, edge, combination;
  point and footprint faults; some settings only in test.
- Protected extremes (large coherent heavy rain, PLAUSIBLE BUT UNVERIFIED) and ambiguous extremes, never corrupted.
- No real RF25 labels exist. Every table carries the label tier.
- Figure: example faults (`fig_example_faults`).

## 4. Components
- Rules, slow (untrained), neighbour-average baseline.
- Spatial network (13x13 patch, centre disc hidden), temporal GRU (own previous 30 days), plain GRU point-forecast baseline.
- Zero-inflated Gamma head; score = surprise (negative log two-sided tail probability). Corruption applied to inputs on the
  fly in training; targets stay clean.

## 5. Fusion and budgets
- Shared logistic fusion (main), mean, OR, boosted trees, per-client, federated-averaged logistic.
- Two budgets (final, review) in false alerts per 1,000 clean cell-days, chosen from validation curves and logged in the
  protocol before any test scoring. Fill in the logged values.
- Figure: recall against budget, validation years (`fig_recall_budget`).

## 6. Federated simulation
- fedpers (main), fedavg, fedft, local; FedProx switch. Matched total gradient steps. Unseen clients get averaged heads;
  local-only has no unseen model (NOT AVAILABLE). One machine: no privacy claim, no communication cost claim.
- Fusion fitted on federated components (`python -m qc fusion --on fedpers|fedavg|fedft`) is implemented and tested on a tiny run; not run on real data.
- Offline comparison (rainnet, scalenet, msnet, lstm_ae) is implemented as an adapter, tested with tiny random-weight artifacts. It is NON-CAUSAL, trained outside this system on all cells, so unseen clients are not scored for it, and a model whose training or validation years overlap any scored year is refused. No real artifact is in the repo.

## 7. Evaluation
- Seen and unseen clients; recall, event recall, false alerts per 1,000 clean cell-days, PR-AUC, footprint IoU, detection
  delay; bootstrap intervals; mean and range over three seeds.
- Calibration against the synthetic fault rate (`fig_reliability`). It does not say anything about real error probability.
- Test years scored once (single-use lock). Stress period evaluation only.
- Case study: Kerala August 2018 (`fig_kerala_case`) from RF25 values and system alerts only. External reference
  totals are UNVERIFIED and are not used until checked against an IMD source.

## 8. UK real-label study (separate)
- MIDAS Open daily rainfall; labels are hand corrections after a failed neighbour check (status 5, estimate 2, query 7).
- Original gridded series scored against corrected. Subset, circularity, undercounted errors and gauge-spread caveats
  print with the table. Never pooled with India. Reader unverified against real files; counties, grid and k are OPEN.

Bars used by the check are in `qc/paper.py` (`BARS`) and are printed in `claims.md`. A claim whose table does not
support it is dropped or reworded; "CANNOT CHECK" means the inputs were not run.

## 10. Limitations to state
- Synthetic faults only for RF25; recall is recall of injected faults.
- Protected extremes are unverified; flagged share is a false-removal rate only if they are genuine.
- Federated results are simulated on one machine.
- Research notes in `docs/research/` marked unverified must be checked at the source before citation.
- Budgets, timing numbers and all real results are still to be produced on the training machine.
