# ERA5 training methodology: verification (2026-11-17)

Method: a deep-research run (5 search angles, 22 sources fetched, 104 claims extracted, 25 verified by three adversarial votes each, 24 kept, 1 rejected). Only claims that survived verification are stated as findings. Items 5 and 6 produced no surviving claim and are open, not confirmed. Two key sources are 2026 arXiv preprints and are not peer reviewed.

| # | Choice in the code | Verdict | What the evidence says | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1a | Download `reanalysis-era5-single-levels` (`qc/era5_fetch.py`, `scripts/download_era5.py`) | **Confirmed** | The CDS dataset is the official 1940-present ERA5; the preliminary 1950-1978 extension (overly intense tropical cyclones) is deprecated and no longer downloadable (Soci et al. 2024; ECMWF forum, Feb 2024). | None for new downloads. Cite Soci et al. 2024. |
| 1b | Climatology and training on 1951-2012 (`era5.years.train`) | **Risky** | ERA5 quality improves through time: about 53,000 assimilated observations a day in 1950, about 570,000 by 1978, about 25 million by 2022 (Soci et al. 2024; Bell et al. 2021). Land 2 m temperature is biased cold before 1967 and humidity looks dry in the 1950s-60s; no station check of humidity before about 1973 (Simmons et al. 2021, ECMWF Tech Memo 881; global or land averages, not India-specific). | **Done (2026-11-17): `era5.years.train` is now 1979-2012.** A check for a break at 1979 in residual mean and spread is still open. The change also removes 28 years from the download. |
| 1c | Daily mean, min, max from 24 hourly instants; ERA5T (`expver`) merged by taking whichever value is present (`qc/era5.py`) | Not verified | No surviving claim. | Standard practice (unverified here): keep ERA5T days (about the last 3 months) out of training and threshold years, since they can be revised. A daily max from hourly instants slightly underestimates a station Tmax. |
| 2 | RH = 100 es(Td)/es(T), Magnus over water, Alduchov-Eskridge constants, clipped to 100 % (`qc/era5.py`) | **Confirmed** | ECMWF's ERA5 guidance defines near-surface RH over water; the IFS diagnoses the 2 m dewpoint over water (WMO practice); earthkit-meteo's RH-from-dewpoint uses water. The mixed water/ice phase is for pressure-level RH only. IFS uses Buck (1981) constants; the difference is well under 1 % in RH. | Optional: switch to the IFS Buck constants (6.1121, 17.502, 240.97). Flag cells below -40 C (outside the formula's recommended range). |
| 3a | IMD rain day ends 03 UTC (08:30 IST) | **Confirmed** | IMD operational documents; RF25 points coincide with ERA5 0.25-degree points. Pai et al. (2014) does not state the day boundary, so do not cite it for this. | Cite IMD operational documents or Rajeevan et al. 2006. |
| 3b | `label_offset_hours: 21` (window 03 UTC D-1 to 03 UTC D labelled D) | **Plausible, thinly sourced** | One preprint (a single 6-day event) shows IMD totals labelled with the end date and a best fit near +20 h. One supporting claim from an IMD press release was rejected (1-2). | Keep the default 0 while the ERA5 path is not linked to RF25. Before linking, lag-correlate ERA5 daily fields (for example ERA5 precipitation aligned with +21 h) against RF25 at -1, 0 and +1 day. |
| 4 | Detector trained on ERA5 grid cells, meant to transfer to AWS | **Risky** | At Hanle (about 4,500 m) ERA5 grid-cell surface pressure is about 555 hPa against about 585 hPa at the site height, a 30 hPa gap from the cell-versus-site height difference (one preprint). | The pipeline already works on standardized anomalies, which removes the mean offset. For any station use, reduce pressure to a common height (hypsometric or MSLP) and correct temperature for height, then validate on real AWS data. |
| 5 | Synthetic faults injected into reanalysis; comparison with WMO, HadISD, ISD and buddy checks | Not verified | No surviving claim. | Open. Compare against the standard tests and validate on real station faults. |
| 6 | Gradient-boosted neighbour regression, masked-target CNN with Gaussian NLL, year splits, false-alarm budgets on clean years | Not verified | No surviving claim. Note: training on 1951-2012 and setting thresholds on 2016-2017 adds to the risk in 1b. Budgets set on ERA5 clean years are not real AWS false-alarm rates. | Open. |
