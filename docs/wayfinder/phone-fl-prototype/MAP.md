# Flutter app: gauge alerts plus single-device on-device training prototype (map)

## Destination

A locked **spec** plus ordered build slices (in `TODO.md`) for one Android-only Flutter app with two features: (1) nearby **gauge alerts** with explanation (rule-based, from the live feed), and (2) a background **on-device training prototype** on one real phone (barometer pressure tendency, tap labels) whose weight updates go to a small free-tier aggregation service. Nothing is built while mapping.

## Notes

- Supersedes the alerts-only plan from the 2026-11-19 chat. Alerts stay, now as feature 1.
- Domain: precipitation QC platform (`backend/`, `frontend/`) and the offline model line (`ml-service/qc`). Vocabulary in `CONTEXT.md`; update it when a term is resolved. Consult the `grilling` and `domain-modeling` skills for decision tickets.
- Standing rules from `AGENTS.md`: no fake functionality; label prototype, synthetic and unbuilt (NOT IMPLEMENTED / PLANNED); no accuracy or "improved" claim without evaluation; the platform and `ml-service/qc` import nothing from each other; secrets in `.env` only.
- Settled with the owner (2026-11-19): demo of a concept, no compliance work, but the app says what it does (one-line disclosure beside the OS permission). Background training is periodic (charging, idle, Wi-Fi), Android only. Only model updates leave the phone, never raw readings or precise location. Everything must work for a free user: free tiers only. Updated 2026-11-19: the owner can install the app on several real devices. With one device "federation" is degenerate (fine-tuning with one client); with a few devices a weighted average is meaningful, but a handful is still not a population, so robust aggregation, secure aggregation and differential privacy stay NOT IMPLEMENTED or PLANNED and the app may not call the setup private or secure. The number of devices is always stated wherever a result is shown. Final 2026-11-19 (owner): 3 real phones in Mumbai, at Bhandup, Goregaon and Dadar, each at a polled gauge once the polled set is changed (see the polled-gauge ticket). The effective federated clients are 3, about 10 to 18 km apart (approximate), and the device count shown with any result is the number that actually contributed. Leave-one-device-out trains on 2 phones, so a federated-versus-local-only comparison is computable (2 clients), but NOT EVALUATED remains the likely status in post-monsoon weather and 3 phones are still a handful, not a population.
- Owner's instruction (2026-11-19): no persistent notification; the user is unaware beyond the OS permission request. Consequence from the research: Android gives background apps no sensor events and a foreground service must show a notification, so barometer readings can only be collected while the app is open in the foreground. Training and upload still run in the background. Collection is therefore sparse (only during app use), and the model, labels and evaluation (t05, t07) must be sized for that. The app does not try to get around the platform restriction.
- **Direction change 2026-11-19 (owner):** drop the phone barometer (the connected phone has none) and use gauge context (temperature, humidity, wind and their changes, time of day) as inputs; then rework the federated part into data validation and anomaly detection. Federating rain prediction on public gauge data gives no privacy benefit (mechanics only); federating a gauge-fault detector would be justified only by custodians who cannot share raw station data, which is what `ml-service/qc` already simulates with IMD sub-division clients. CORRECTED by the research ticket: the sources support restrictions on redistributing some data (NOAA ISD readme; IMD third-party sharing rule; WMO Resolution 40 only on commercial re-export), not a general "cannot share" rule, and nothing found addresses model updates leaving a custodian. The rain-soon model, barometer inputs and 1-minute window design are superseded pending the federated-validation design ticket; the update protocol and Worker, the simulated clients (as simulated custodians), the polled-gauge set and the alerts app survive; the evaluation rules need rewriting for fault-detection metrics.
- Owner answers 2026-11-19: net is a small temporal neural net (not the 5 to 8 to 1 net); nearest-gauge context features are wanted (the gauge's own rain fields are never inputs, because the label comes from them); simulated clients are in scope. **Sensor check by adb on the one connected phone (a Moto G67 Power 5G): it has NO barometer, NO ambient-temperature and NO humidity sensor** (it has accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, light, proximity and step sensors). The owner said to assume the same for the other phones. Consequence: the barometer-based phone signal is not available on these phones, so t05 is reopened (see its addendum); the other phones should be checked before assuming.
- The phone-trained model is a NEW small model (rain-onset from pressure tendency, tap labels). The RF25 QC weights cannot take phone data and are not claimed to improve. Confirm after the research tickets.
- Alerts side: SUSPECT verdicts from the live Weather Union feed (9 Mumbai gauges, units UNCONFIRMED, time basis received), baseline explanation marked generated; UNCERTAIN in a separate weaker list; never "anomaly" or "fault" in app copy.
- Tracker: local markdown. Tickets are `tickets/*.md`; frontmatter holds `status`, `assignee`, `blocked_by`. Research notes go in `docs/research/`.
- User preference: file changes only with the Edit/Write tools, never shell rewrites.

## Decisions so far

- [Is `/api/alerts` a new endpoint or a view over the existing ones, and what does it return?](tickets/t08-alerts-contract.md): a new `GET /api/alerts` backed by `backend/app/validation/alerts.py`, one call from the app; only the latest non-synthetic live run, stale after two poll intervals; 10 km default and 25 km maximum radius (assumed), at most 20 alerts, SUSPECT in `alerts` and UNCERTAIN in a separate `unverified` list, `gauges_in_range` reported; explanation fetched on open through the existing evidence and baseline explain calls; coordinates rounded to 2 decimals by the app and never stored; every normal condition is a 200 with a `state`; every response carries prototype, time-basis, units-UNCONFIRMED and Mumbai-only flags.

- [What free tiers can host the aggregation service and the weights storage?](tickets/t03-free-tier-hosting-research.md): research only, no choice made yet; Render free can serve but not store (sleeps after 15 minutes, ephemeral disk); Cloudflare Workers plus R2 is the best documented free fit, with payment-method and terms UNVERIFIED; Fly.io and Koyeb are not free for a new user; Hugging Face is contradictory in its own docs.

- [Can a Flutter Android app train a small model on the device, and with what framework?](tickets/t02-on-device-training-research.md): research only, no choice made yet; training is documented in LiteRT and ONNX Runtime (the claim that no Flutter package trains was corrected by the framework research: `flutter_litert` documents it, unverified until run); `ml-service/qc` is PyTorch so weights need an export step; periodic work is 15 minutes at best and may be skipped; Android 9 and later give background apps no sensor events, so barometer collection needs the foreground or a foreground service, and the "unaware" part of the design covers only training and upload.

- [What does existing research say about predicting rain from phone barometer pressure tendency?](tickets/t01-barometer-rain-onset-research.md): research only; WMO tendency is a 3-hour quantity, so shorter windows are our own and named differently; phone barometers are good on relative change and poor on absolute level, and floor changes mimic weather; no primary source found giving rain-onset skill from one device's tendency, so the prototype needs its own baseline and evaluation and no skill can be assumed.

- [What does the federated-update literature say about aggregation, poisoned updates and the single-client case?](tickets/t04-federated-update-protocol-research.md): research only; with one client FedAvg is plain fine-tuning (derived); norm clipping is a sanity check and not security; robust aggregation, secure aggregation and differential privacy are meaningless with one device and stay PLANNED, and the app may not call the setup private or secure; no source on barometer or rain-onset federated learning; no update validation exists anywhere in the repo yet.

- [What model, features and labels does the phone train?](tickets/t05-model-features-labels.md): a new separate rain-soon model (prototype), not the QC weights; target is rain at the nearest gauge within 60 minutes, labelled from the gauge's counter increase and only when that gauge is within 10 km and its verdict is OK, no taps; features are short pressure change over 10, 20 and 30 minutes plus slope and standard deviation from one-minute foreground samples (30 contiguous minutes needed, step changes rejected); a 5 to 8 to 1 neural net with 57 parameters from a fixed seeded random start; training code (hand-written or a framework) waits on the framework research; rounds close at 2 or more updates (assumed); a phone trains only with 50 or more labelled windows of both classes; must be compared with a plain baseline before any claim.

- [How is the phone model evaluated and what may we claim?](tickets/t07-evaluation-and-claims.md): rules frozen in a new `docs/phone-model-evaluation-protocol.md` before any run; leave-one-device-out with earlier-to-later validation on the training phones, each held-out phone scored once; four mandatory comparisons (no-rain base rate, fixed pressure-fall threshold, logistic regression, local-only training); precision, recall, F1, false alarms per 1,000 negatives and precision-recall area, with NOT EVALUATED when pooled positives are under 30 or a held-out phone has under 5 (likely in post-monsoon November); "better than baseline" only if a by-day bootstrap interval at a fixed false-alarm rate excludes zero; a Not-claimed list; synthetic data only for tests and never in a reported metric.

- [Which 9 gauges does the live feed poll, given where the phones are?](tickets/t11-polled-gauge-set.md): Goregaon East and West, Kandivali East, Andheri West and East, Malad West, Powai, Bhandup West plus Dadar West, with Mulund West dropped (the author's choice, open to change); final phones at Bhandup, Goregaon and Dadar (3 clients); Dadar West has no polled neighbour within 10 km so its neighbour check is "not run" and its label guard rests on rules alone; build needs an explicit station list instead of the automatic cluster; nothing deployed was changed while mapping.

- [Which mobile federated-learning frameworks exist, and which fits an Android prototype on free tiers?](tickets/t10-mobile-fl-frameworks-research.md): research only, no choice made yet; `flutter_litert` 4.2.0 documents on-device training in Flutter (young, unverified until built), Flower's Android SDK is still unreleased, NVFlare has an Android client but is heavy and native-only, FedKit, FedML, TensorFlow Federated, Google federated compute and PySyft are not usable; derived conclusion: `flutter_litert` plus a plain HTTP upload plus a server-side weighted mean covers 3 phones and 57 parameters, and leaving Flutter gains no ready-made client except NVFlare.

- [What is the update format, aggregation rule, validation and weights storage?](tickets/t06-update-aggregation-storage.md): a Cloudflare Worker (TypeScript) with D1, no R2; `fedupdate-v1` JSON with a norm-clipped 57-float delta, 64 KB cap, no readings, coordinates or gauge IDs; one update per device per version, rounds close at 2 or more devices (assumed) with a window-weighted mean of deltas, stale updates get 409; an allowlist of 3 per-phone token secrets; a committed `model-spec-v1` with fixed scaling constants and seeded initial weights, spec hash checked by the phone; idempotent uploads; evaluation happens on the phone and only per-day outcome counts (`eval-v1`) leave, so only updates and aggregate counts leave the phone; every response is labelled prototype with the device count.

- [How are many simulated clients generated, run and kept honest next to the 3 real phones?](tickets/t12-simulated-clients.md): on synthetic data, federated versus local-only versus pooled, uneven clients, dropouts and poisoned updates; 50 simulated clients (sweeps at 10 and 100), 50 rounds, 20% sampled per round, 3 seeds; generator B (trend, twice-daily swing, noise, per-client bias, step artefacts, lagged rain onset, all assumed) with a mandatory null control that must show no advantage; sweeps in-process plus a small run through the real Worker on `sim-` lineages kept apart from the real one; new NumPy reference in `ml-service/fed-sim/` leaving `federated.py` alone; four poisoning attacks (the label flip passes the norm clip, and the report says so); real ISD replay is a separate proxy study (3-hour tendency, not the phone model); everything labelled synthetic or proxy, never counted toward a rain-prediction claim.

- [What does prior work say about federated learning for weather-station data validation and anomaly detection, and who could be the clients?](tickets/t13-federated-weather-qc-research.md): research only; prior work exists for each ingredient (FedLGAN on 4 hydrological stations with injected noise, FedLSTM on temperature) so no novelty claim, and none found federating gauge QC on real fault labels with custodians as clients; the "custodians cannot share raw data" premise is only partly supported (ISD readme, IMD third-party rule, Resolution 40 only on commercial re-export) and nothing addresses model updates leaving a custodian; operational consistency checks are fixed logic (dewpoint, gust, zero-duration or zero-cloud precipitation) and no humidity-versus-rain rule was found.
